Seed garment photos.

These are the project owner's own downloaded product shots, used to populate the
demo closet. They stand in for a real wardrobe: the app needs an actual picture
of a garment for virtual try-on, which a flat colour swatch cannot provide.

Selection was not arbitrary. Each was run through the same centre-crop and
colour-bucketing that DominantColor applies, and only photos whose extracted
colour matched the garment were kept. Rejected examples and why:

  navy chinos    a model against a bright orange wall — the wall dominates the
                 crop and, being chromatic, survives the background filter, so
                 the extractor returned orange
  white tshirt   white on white; almost nothing survives the filter
  beige pants    washed out, too pale to read as a colour
